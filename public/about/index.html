关于本站

About this project

让值得阅读的内容，更容易抵达。 Making valuable ideas easier to reach.

DawnNav（黎明导航）是一个开放的知识导航项目，持续整理新闻、出版物、杂志、博客与社交媒体等网络资源。

DawnNav is an open knowledge directory that organizes online resources including news, publications, magazines, blogs, and social media.

我们的使命

Our mission

连接分散的知识

Connecting scattered knowledge

网络上有大量有价值的资料，却常常分散在不同语言、平台和地区。DawnNav 希望通过清晰的分类、简洁的介绍和可搜索的目录，降低发现这些内容的门槛。

本站重点收录马克思主义、社会主义、工人运动及相关社会议题的资料，同时欢迎读者提供新的站点、信息修正与使用建议。

Valuable material is often scattered across languages, platforms, and regions. DawnNav uses clear categories, concise descriptions, and a searchable directory to make that material easier to discover.

The directory focuses on Marxism, socialism, labor movements, and related social issues. Suggestions for new resources, corrections, and improvements are always welcome.

收录原则

Editorial principles

一个可靠目录应当做到什么

What a useful directory should do

01

清晰

Clarity

用明确的分类和简短说明，帮助读者快速判断资源是否适合自己。

Clear categories and concise notes help readers quickly understand each resource.

02

开放

Openness

尊重不同语言与地区的资料来源，并持续接受社区的补充和纠正。

We welcome sources from different languages and regions, along with community corrections.

03

审慎

Care

尽力维护链接和简介，但鼓励读者在访问第三方网站前自行核实信息。

We maintain links and descriptions with care, while encouraging readers to verify third-party information.
